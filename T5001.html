<h1 property="name" id="wb-cont" class="gc-thickline">Completing Form T5001</h1>


</div>
<div class="mwsbodytext text parbase section">

<div class="row"><div class="col-md-8"><p>As a tax shelter promoter, you have to use this form to apply for a tax shelter identification number. If a tax shelter has more than one promoter, only one promoter has to apply. However, you have to give information about the principal promoter.</p>
<h2>On this page</h2>
<ul>
<li><a href="#h_1">Mandatory information</a></li>
<li><a href="#detailed_inst">Detailed instructions</a></li>
<li><a href="#Send">Where to send the completed T5001</a></li>
</ul>
<h2 id="h_1">Mandatory information</h2>
<p>You have to complete <strong>all</strong> the sections on <a href="/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/forms/t5001.html">Form T5001, Application for Tax Shelter Identification Number and Undertaking to Keep Books and Records</a>. If there is not enough space for any section, attach a separate list or sheet. If you do not enter all the information the Canada Revenue Agency (CRA) asks for in each section, or do not send all the documents the CRA requires, the CRA may contact you to get the missing information. Missing information can delay our issuing a tax shelter identification number.</p>
<div class="alert alert-info"><p>Full disclosure is required of all documents relating to this application. All the information requested in Form T5001 and in the documents supporting your application&nbsp;is &quot;prescribed information.&quot; If the information you provide for any section is insufficient, your application will be treated as invalid and the issuing of the tax shelter identification number may be delayed, or even refused.</p>
</div>
<p>The promoter has to provide the CRA with prescribed information on the application, including:</p>
<ul>
<li>references to the <strong>precise</strong> location of each item of information (for example, responses such as &quot;see prospectus&quot; are incomplete—you have to provide the precise annex number, page number, and paragraph or clause reference)</li>
<li>a copy of an offering memorandum</li>
<li>a statement that the promoter will keep the books and records in Canada</li>
</ul>
</div>
</div>



</div>
<div class="mwsbodytext text parbase section">

<div class="row"><div class="col-md-8"><h2 id="detailed_inst">Detailed instructions</h2>
<div class="mwsaccordion-html section"><button type="button" class="btn btn-default wb-toggle mrgn-bttm-md" data-toggle="{&quot;selector&quot;: &quot;details&quot;, &quot;parent&quot;: &quot;#details-elements-30143&quot;, &quot;type&quot;: &quot;on&quot;}">Expand all</button> <button type="button" class="btn btn-default wb-toggle mrgn-bttm-md" data-toggle="{&quot;selector&quot;: &quot;details&quot;, &quot;parent&quot;: &quot;#details-elements-30143&quot;, &quot;type&quot;: &quot;off&quot;}">Collapse all</button><ul class="list-unstyled" id="details-elements-30143">
<li><details><summary>Section A – Identification information</summary><div class="mwsbodytext text parbase section"><h3>Tax shelter</h3>
<p><strong>Tax shelter's name and address</strong>&nbsp;– Enter the name and complete address of the tax shelter entity in the left block of <span class="nowrap">Section A</span>. If the tax shelter consists of activities and there is no tax shelter entity, enter the principal promoter's name and complete address instead.</p>
<p id="address"><strong><a id="complete_addr"></a>Complete address</strong>&nbsp;– Wherever a complete address is requested on the application <a href="/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/forms/t5001.html">Form T5001, Application for Tax Shelter Identification Number and Undertaking to Keep Books and Records</a>, use the following codes:</p>
<ul>
<li><strong>Province or territory</strong>&nbsp;– Use the two-letter code from &quot;Appendix A&nbsp;– Canadian province or&nbsp;territory,&nbsp;or&nbsp;U.S. state, territory, or possession codes&quot; in the <a href="/en/revenue-agency/services/forms-publications/publications/t4068.html">T4068, Guide&nbsp;for the Partnership Information Return&nbsp;(T5013 forms)</a>, for the name of the Canadian province or territory. For all other countries, leave this area blank.</li>
<li><strong>Postal code</strong>&nbsp;– Enter the Canadian postal code.</li>
<li><strong>Country</strong>&nbsp;– Enter &quot;CAN&quot; or the appropriate three-letter code from &quot;Appendix B&nbsp;– Country codes&quot; in the T4068<em> </em>guide, for any other country.</li>
<li><strong>State</strong>&nbsp;– Use the two-letter code from &quot;Appendix A&nbsp;– Canadian province or&nbsp;territory,&nbsp;or U.S. state, territory, or possession codes&quot; in the T4068 guide, for the name of the U.S. state. For all other countries, use the two-letter code for your state or province if it has one.</li>
<li><strong>U.S. zip code</strong>&nbsp;– Enter the <span class="nowrap">U.S. zip</span> code. For all other countries (except Canada), use the postal code if that country has one. Otherwise, leave this area blank.</li>
</ul>
<p><strong>Contact person's name and telephone number</strong>&nbsp;– Enter the name and daytime telephone number of the person the CRA can contact if they need more information about the tax shelter. If the tax shelter consists of activities and there is no tax shelter entity, enter the principal promoter's daytime telephone number.</p>
</div>
<div class="mwsbodytext text parbase section"><h3>Principal promoter</h3>
<p><strong>Principal promoter's name, address, and telephone number</strong>&nbsp;– Enter the name, complete address, and daytime telephone number of the tax shelter's principal promoter in the right block of <span class="nowrap">Section A</span>. Follow the instructions in <a href="#complete_addr">Complete address</a>.</p>
<p><strong>Identification number</strong>&nbsp;– Enter the principal promoter's identification number:</p>
<ul>
<li>for an <strong>individual</strong>, enter the social insurance number</li>
<li>for a <strong>partnership</strong>, enter the partnership's account number or the business number (BN) for that partnership</li>
<li>for a <strong>trust</strong>, enter the trust account number for that trust</li>
<li>for a <strong>corporation</strong>, enter a &quot;0&quot; followed by the corporation's BN</li>
</ul>

<div class="mwsbodytext text parbase section"><p><strong><a id="Arms_length"></a>Arm's length</strong> refers to a relationship or a transaction between persons who act in their separate interests. An arm’s length transaction is generally a transaction that reflects ordinary commercial dealings between parties acting in their separate interests.</p>
<p><strong>Related persons</strong> are not considered to deal with each other at arm’s length. Related persons include individuals connected by blood relationship, marriage, common-law partnership or adoption (legal or in fact). A corporation and another person or two corporations may also be related persons.</p>
<p><strong>Unrelated persons</strong> may not be dealing with each other at arm’s length at a particular time. Each case will depend upon its own facts. The following criteria will be considered to determine whether parties to a transaction are not dealing at arm’s length:</p>
<ul>
<li>whether there is a common mind that directs the bargaining for the parties to a transaction</li>
<li>whether the parties to a transaction act in concert without separate interests; &quot;acting in concert&quot; means, for example, that parties act with considerable interdependence on a transaction of common interest</li>
<li>whether there is de facto control of one party by the other because of, for example, advantage, authority or influence</li>
</ul>
<p>For&nbsp;more information, see the <a href="/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/technical-information/income-tax/income-tax-folios-index/series-1-individuals/folio-5-transfer-income-property-rights-third-parties/income-tax-folio-s1-f5-c1-related-persons-dealing-arms-length.html">Income tax folio S1-F5-C1, Related persons and dealing at arm's length</a>.</p>
<p><strong>Non arm’s length</strong> generally refers to a relationship or transaction between persons who are related to each other.</p>
<p>However, a non-arm’s length relationship might also exist between unrelated individuals, partnerships or corporations, depending on the circumstances. For more information, see the definition of <a href="#Arms_length">Arm’s length</a>.</p>

<div class="mwsbodytext text parbase section"><p><strong>Vendor's name and address </strong>–<strong>&nbsp;</strong>Enter the vendor's name and complete address for each transaction concerning the purchase and sale of property in the tax shelter. Follow the instructions in section&nbsp;<a href="#complete_addr">Complete address</a>. Include all entities involved in the purchase and sale of the property in the tax shelter.</p>
</div>
<div class="mwspanel section"><section class="panel panel-default"><header class="panel-heading"><h3 class="panel-title">Note</h3>
</header><div class="panel-body"><div class="mwsbodytext text parbase section"><p>An entity is involved in the tax shelter if, by their absence, the tax shelter arrangement would not provide the benefits expected by the participant.</p>
</div>
</div>
</section></div>
<div class="mwsbodytext text parbase section"><p><strong>Description of property and cost to purchaser </strong>–&nbsp;Enter the description and cost to the purchaser of the property for each transaction concerning the purchase and sale of property in the tax shelter.</p>
<p>A transaction includes all of the factual elements relevant to the expected tax treatment of any investment, entity, plan, or arrangement, and includes any series of steps carried out as part of a plan.</p>
</div>
<div class="mwsbodytext text parbase section"><p id="cost"><strong>Valuation or appraisal to determine cost? </strong>–<strong>&nbsp;</strong>For each purchase, <span class="nowrap">tick (✔)</span> the appropriate column. If you answer <strong>yes</strong>, fill out&nbsp;<span class="nowrap">Section J&nbsp;–</span> Valuation and appraisal information.</p>
</div>
<div class="mwsbodytext text parbase section"><p><strong>Additional information required </strong>–<strong>&nbsp;</strong>If the principal promoter or an entity not dealing at arm's length with the principal promoter purchased the property, provide the information on the arm's length vendors, and attach a copy of the purchase agreement. If property to be sold to the tax shelter investors is being held by arm's length vendors, provide the information on the vendors and attach a draft purchase agreement.</p>
<p>For more information about arm's length, see the <a href="/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/technical-information/income-tax/income-tax-folios-index/series-1-individuals/folio-5-transfer-income-property-rights-third-parties/income-tax-folio-s1-f5-c1-related-persons-dealing-arms-length.html">Income tax folio S1-F5-C1, Related Persons and Dealing at Arm’s Length</a>.</p>
<p>If there is not enough space, attach a separate sheet using the same format as on this form.</p>
</div>
</details></li>
<li><details><summary>Section J – Valuation and appraisal information</summary><div class="mwsbodytext text parbase section"><p>If you answer <strong>yes</strong> in <span class="nowrap">question 6</span> in <span class="nowrap">Section C</span>, or to the question &quot;Valuation or appraisal to determine cost?&quot; in <span class="nowrap">Section I</span>, you have to fill out&nbsp;<span class="nowrap">Section J</span> and provide the additional information requested below.</p>
</div>
<div class="mwsbodytext text parbase section"><p><strong>Valuator's name and address </strong>–&nbsp;Enter the name and complete address for each valuator involved, or that will be involved in the arrangement. Follow the instructions in section&nbsp;<a href="#complete_addr">Complete address</a>. Include the valuators and appraisers who are expected to prepare reports if the reports have not been prepared yet.</p>
<p>If the arrangement is dependent on a valuation, but a valuation has <strong>not</strong> been made yet and you do not know who the valuator will be, fill out&nbsp;<span class="nowrap">Section L&nbsp;–</span> Outstanding items providing an explanation.</p>
</div>
<div class="mwsbodytext text parbase section"><p><strong>Number of reports </strong>–&nbsp;Enter the number of valuation and appraisal reports prepared by each valuator. Attach a copy of each report, or a draft if final copies are not available, to this application. Send the final copies when they become available.</p>
<p>If no valuations have been made yet but the valuators are known, enter &quot;nil&quot; in this column, and when the reports become available, send a copy to the CRA.</p>
</div>
<div class="mwspanel section"><section class="panel panel-default"><header class="panel-heading"><h2 class="panel-title">Notes</h2>
</header><div class="panel-body"><div class="mwsbodytext text parbase section"><p>Fair market value (FMV)&nbsp;generally means the highest price that a property would bring, expressed in dollars, in an open and unrestricted market, between a willing buyer and a willing seller who are both knowledgeable, informed, and prudent, and who are acting independently of each other.</p>
<p>FMV does <strong>not</strong> include any amounts paid or payable to other parties, such as commissions to sales agents or sales taxes like the goods and services tax/harmonized sales tax (GST/HST) or provincial sales taxes.</p>
</div>
</div>
</section></div>
<div class="mwsbodytext text parbase section"><p>If there is not enough space, attach a separate sheet using the same format as on this form.</p>
</div>
</details></li>
<li><details><summary>Section K – Promotional and other information</summary><div class="mwsbodytext text parbase section"><p class="h4Size"><strong>Will the following material&nbsp;be used to promote the sale of the tax shelter? </strong>–&nbsp;For each item in the list, <span class="nowrap">tick (✔)</span> the appropriate box.</p>
</div>
<div class="mwsbodytext text parbase section"><p>If you answer <strong>yes</strong> for any item, attach all documents (or a draft if final copies are not available) to the application, and send the final copies when they become available.</p>
<p>If you will be making presentations to potential participants in the tax shelter arrangement in which you will be using overhead, electronic or other presentation materials, send a paper copy of the presentation with this application.</p>
<p>If there is not enough space, attach a separate sheet using the same format as on this form.</p>
</div>
</details></li>
<li><details><summary>Section L – Outstanding items</summary><div class="mwsbodytext text parbase section"><p>Fill out this section to provide an explanation why <strong>any</strong> section of this form was not completed, or why any documents the CRA requested have not been provided. For example, if when you complete:</p>
<ul>
<li><span class="nowrap">Section B</span>, you entered <span class="nowrap"><strong>code 99</strong></span><strong> </strong>in <span class="nowrap">question 5</span>, &quot;Expected deductions in the first four years to the purchaser of a minimum investment unit in the tax shelter&quot;,&nbsp;describe the expected deduction<br>
&nbsp;</li>
<li><span class="nowrap">Section C</span>, you enter <span class="nowrap"><strong>code 49</strong></span> in <span class="nowrap">question 2</span>, &quot;Expected amount of donation receipts to be issued to the purchaser of a minimum investment unit&quot;, describe the expected credit or deduction<br>
&nbsp;</li>
<li><span class="nowrap">Section C</span>, you answer <strong>no </strong>in <span class="nowrap">question 6</span>, &quot;Was a valuation or appraisal report used to support the value used on acquisition or donation?&quot;,&nbsp;explain how the value of the gift will be determined<br>
&nbsp;</li>
<li><span class="nowrap">Section C</span>, you answer <strong>Other</strong> in <span class="nowrap">question 7</span>, &quot;How will the donor acquire the property?&quot;,&nbsp;describe how the donor will acquire the property<br>
&nbsp;</li>
<li><span class="nowrap">Section J</span>, you know that the arrangement is dependent on a valuation, but a valuation has not been made yet and you do not know who the valuator will be, provide an explanation<br>
&nbsp;</li>
<li><span class="nowrap">Section J</span>, a valuation has not been made yet but the valuators <strong>are known</strong>, provide an explanation and send the CRA a copy when the reports become available<br>
&nbsp;</li>
<li><span class="nowrap">Section M</span>, you are unable to provide copies of each document, explain why you are unable to do so</li>
</ul>
<p>&nbsp;</p>
</div>
<div class="mwspanel section"><section class="panel panel-default"><header class="panel-heading"><h2 class="panel-title">Note</h2>
</header><div class="panel-body"><div class="mwsbodytext text parbase section"><p>If you do not enter all the information the CRA asks for in <strong>each</strong> section, or do not send all the documents the CRA requires, and you do not fill out&nbsp;<span class="nowrap">Section L</span> to provide an explanation, the CRA may decline to issue a tax shelter identification number.</p>
</div>
</div>
</section></div>
</details></li>
<li><details><summary>Section M – Summary of arrangements</summary><div class="mwsbodytext text parbase section"><p>Fill out this section to provide an explanation for the following situations:</p>
<ul>
<li>If you answered <strong>yes</strong> in <span class="nowrap">question 6</span> in <span class="nowrap">Section B</span>, describe the nature of any amount or benefit listed, including the timing, the parties involved, and whether they deal at non-arm's length with the buyer or the promoter</li>
<li>If you answered <strong>no</strong> in <span class="nowrap">question 8</span> in <span class="nowrap">Section C</span>, describe how the recipients will be involved, and what responsibilities they will have in the arrangement. Include any documents that were presented to the recipients to explain the arrangement, or the involvement of the recipient in the arrangement</li>
<li>If you answered <strong>yes</strong> to the question &quot;Is there an application to become a beneficiary of the trust?&quot; in <span class="nowrap">Section H</span>, but there is no application, provide a description of how a person becomes a beneficiary and what the criteria are for accepting them</li>
<li>Provide a brief overview of the arrangement (1-<span class="nowrap">2 pages</span>) as described in the next section</li>
</ul>
<p>If there is not enough space, attach a separate sheet using the same format as on this form.</p>
</div>
<div class="mwsbodytext text parbase section"><h3>Overview of the arrangement</h3>
<p>The information in the brief overview of the arrangement must be sufficient so the CRA can comprehend the manner in which the arrangements are intended to operate. This should be a straightforward description highlighting:</p>
<ul>
<li>the aim of the arrangements</li>
<li>the entities involved and the relationships, contractual or otherwise, between the entities</li>
<li>the steps involved and the tax law, if any, which the arrangements rely on</li>
</ul>
<p>Where complex transactions are involved, include a flowchart of the arrangements and parties involved. For example, any proposal for any promoter-preferred financing should be disclosed.</p>
<p>Include information explaining each &quot;element&quot; of the proposed arrangements from which the expected tax advantage obtained under those arrangements arises (including the way in which the arrangements are structured).</p>
<p>Provide references to the <strong>precise</strong> location of each item of information (for example, responses such as &quot;see prospectus&quot; are incomplete—you have to provide the precise annex number, page number, and paragraph or clause reference).</p>
</div>
<div class="mwsbodytext text parbase section"><h3>What is an element?</h3>
<p>An element is &quot;any element of the arrangements (including the way in which they are structured) from which the tax advantage expected to be obtained arises.&quot; An element includes, but is not limited to:</p>
<ul>
<li>a financial product</li>
<li>a way of combining that financial product with other elements of the arrangements</li>
<li>a way of interpreting the tax legislation in relation to the financial arrangements, that is, the tax analysis which underlies the structuring of the arrangements</li>
<li>the responsibilities and duties of each person involved in the arrangement</li>
<li>the benefits to be obtained</li>
<li>the method for participation in the arrangement</li>
<li>any agreements or proposed agreements between the arrangement entities and the applicant (or any entity associated with applicant) including loans, subcontracting arrangements, arrangements relating to the acquisition of assets and the distribution of profits arising from the arrangement</li>
</ul>
<p>You have to provide copies of each document with the application. If you are unable to provide a document, fill out&nbsp;<span class="nowrap">Section L&nbsp;–</span> Outstanding items to explain why you are unable to do so. Where available, also provide copies of the following documents:</p>
<ul>
<li>management agreements</li>
<li>loan agreements and other finance documentation</li>
<li>marketing materials and any promotional material issued or to be issued to participants, financial planners or advisors or commission agents</li>
<li>any written information to be provided to participants regarding their investment</li>
<li>any other agreements or contracts including purchase agreements, rental agreements, franchise agreements</li>
<li>flow chart(s) detailing the flow of funds (as amounts per participant) between the participant, promoter, financier, trustee, manager, and any other entity in receipt of the investment funds (including directors, officers, shareholders, partners and beneficiaries of these entities, as well as their residency status)</li>
<li>working papers and any other materials used in setting the fees payable by a participant</li>
</ul>
<p>If not included in the documents, describe:</p>
<ul>
<li>the training or expertise of the persons involved with the activity</li>
<li>your plan to make the activity profitable</li>
<li>the capability of the venture as capitalized to show a profit</li>
</ul>
</div>
</details></li>
<li><details><summary>Section N – Undertaking to keep books and records</summary><div class="mwsbodytext text parbase section"><p>You have to fill out the undertaking and sign it. If the promoter does not sign the undertaking, the CRA will <strong>not</strong> issue a tax shelter identification number. The promoter's (or authorized person's) signature must be an original signature for the CRA to process the application.</p>
<p>The CRA will issue a tax shelter identification number <strong>only when</strong> they receive all the prescribed information, <strong>and if</strong> they are satisfied with the undertaking to keep the tax shelter’s books and records.</p>
</div>
<div class="mwspanel section"><section class="panel panel-default"><header class="panel-heading"><h2 class="panel-title">Notes</h2>
</header><div class="panel-body"><div class="mwsbodytext text parbase section"><p>An undertaking by a person, who proposes to sell or issue interests in a tax shelter, to keep the requisite books and records at that person’s normal place of business in Canada will normally be satisfactory.</p>
<p>An example of an unacceptable undertaking would be if the promoter's place of business is in Toronto, Ontario but the books and records are maintained in Whitehorse, Yukon Territory, as Whitehorse is not the promoter's place of business, even though it is in Canada.</p>
</div>
</div>
</section></div>
</details></li>
<li><details><summary>Section O – Certification</summary><div class="mwsbodytext text parbase section"><p>Form T5001 must be signed and dated by the tax shelter promoter.</p>
</div>
</details></li>
</ul>
